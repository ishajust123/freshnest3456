The problem

We bin 9% of our fresh food because stores order by gut feeling.

Our solution

Smarter daily orders, private app offers for food about to expire, and a live scoreboard.

The result

Waste falls to 5% in 12 months and saves Rs. 24 crore a year. The Rs. 6 crore comes back in about 9 months if we cut 4 points.
